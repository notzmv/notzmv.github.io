* Introduction & Overview
*Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim* (In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful).

**Aljamiado Português** is a personal, highly systematic orthographic and phonetic adaptation of the Perso-Arabic script designed to write the Portuguese language. Historically, *Aljamiado* (from Arabic *'ajamiyya*, "non-Arabic / foreign language written in Arabic script") referred to Romance languages (Mozarabic, Spanish, Ladino) transcribed using Arabic letterforms.

This specification formalizes the orthographic engine for Portuguese. The system prioritizes:
1. **Morphophonemic Clarity**: Distinguishing nominal plural inflection from verbal agreement and lexical root sibilants.
2. **Visual Rhythm & Balance**: Utilizing a graphic plural suffix (*-hā* / `ـها`) to prevent long, repetitive *sīn* (`س`) tails from cluttering horizontal text flow.
3. **Phonetic & Graphic Precision**: Differentiating soft *c* (`چ`) from soft *g/j* (`ژ`), single *r* (`ر`) from double *rr* (`ه`), intervocalic *s* (`ز`) from double *ss* (`س`), establishing explicit Madd rules for initial vowels, and leveraging **Tonic Alif** (`ا`) for stress accentuation.

** Soft C vs. Soft G/J Distinction
To maintain precise graphic differentiation between Romance roots:
- **Soft C** (before *e*, *i*) is written with **Chā** (`چ`):
  - *cenoura* $\rightarrow$ `چنوره`
  - *você* $\rightarrow$ `ووچه` / `وچه`
- **Soft G / J** (before *e*, *i*) is written with **Žā** (`ژ`):
  - *gente* $\rightarrow$ `ژنته`
  - *projeto* $\rightarrow$ `پرژتو`
  - *gostei* $\rightarrow$ `گوستى` (hard *g* uses **Gāf** `گ`)

** R vs. RR Distinction (Rā vs. He)
Rhotics are strictly divided based on articulation weight:
- **Single intervocalic or tap/flap *r*** [ɾ] maps to **Rā** (`ر`):
  - *caro* $\rightarrow$ `كارو`
  - *para* $\rightarrow$ `پارا`
  - *escrever* $\rightarrow$ `إسكرور`
- **Double *rr* or strong word-initial *r-*** [ʁ] maps to **He** (`ه` / `ـه`):
  - *carro* $\rightarrow$ `كاهو`
  - *rio* $\rightarrow$ `هيو`
  - *rua* $\rightarrow$ `هوه`

** Intervocalic S vs. SS Contrast (Zāy vs. Sīn)
In intervocalic positions where Latin orthography distinguishes voicing:
- **Single intervocalic *s*** (voiced /z/) maps to **Zāy** (`ز`):
  - *coisa* $\rightarrow$ `كويزه`
  - *casa* $\rightarrow$ `كازه`
- **Double intervocalic *ss*** (voiceless /s/) maps to **Sīn** (`س`):
  - *processo* $\rightarrow$ `پروچسو`
  - *passo* $\rightarrow$ `پاسو`

** Palatal Digraphs (lh, nh)
Palatal consonants are rendered as consonant-glide ligatures using *Lām-Yā* (`لي`) and *Nūn-Yā* (`ني`):
- **lh** [ʎ] $\rightarrow$ **لي**: *filho* $\rightarrow$ `فيليو`, *espelho* $\rightarrow$ `اسپليو`
- **nh** [ɲ] $\rightarrow$ **ني**: *minha* $\rightarrow$ `مينيه`, *tenho* $\rightarrow$ `تينيو`

* 2. Vowel System, Diacritics & Accentuation

** Word-Final Vowels (He vs. Waw)
- **Final *-a* and *-e*** both map to **He** (`ه` / `ـه`):
  - *boa* $\rightarrow$ `بوه`
  - *gente* $\rightarrow$ `ژنته`
  - *prática* $\rightarrow$ `پراكتيكه`
- **Final *-o*** maps to **Waw** (`و` / `ـو`):
  - *livro* $\rightarrow$ `ليورو`
  - *como* $\rightarrow$ `كومو`

** Initial Vowel Carriers & Madd Diacritics
When a word begins with a vowel, **Alif** (`ا`) serves as the graphic carrier. Specific open and stressed vowels use **Madd**:

| Initial Sound | Graphic Representation | Diacritic Detail | Example Word | Aljamiado Transliteration |
|---------------+------------------------+------------------+--------------+---------------------------|
| **Initial open é** [ɛ] | **إ̷** / **إي** | Alif with subscript Madd (Fallback: *Alif-Yā* `إي`) | *é* | `إ̷` / `إي` |
| **Initial open à** [a] | **آ** | Alif with superscript Madd | *à* | `آ` |
| **Initial unstressed e** [i]/[e] | **إ** | Alif with Kasrah | *estudo* | `إستودو` |
| **Initial a** [a] | **ا** | Plain Alif | *alfabeto* | `الفبتو` |
| **Initial o** [o]/[u] | **او** | Alif-Waw | *os* | `وها` |

** Tonic Alif (ا) Stress Accentuation
**Alif** (`ا`) functions as a **tonic marker** denoting primary word stress whenever it is not serving as an initial vowel carrier:
- Inserted after the tonic vowel/syllable to explicitly signal stress position.
- *analisar* $\rightarrow$ `اناليزار` (stress on final syllable *-zar*)
- *prática* $\rightarrow$ `پراكتيكه` (stress on initial syllable *prá-*)

** Open vs. Closed Vowel Contrasts (avô vs. avó)
Vowel quality contrasts in mid-vowels leverage Alif vs. Waw arrangements:
- **avô** [ɐ'vu] (masculine grandfather, closed *-ô*) $\rightarrow$ **أوو** (Alif Waw Waw) or `أووه`
- **avó** [ɐ'vɔ] (feminine grandmother, open *-ó*) $\rightarrow$ **أوا** (Alif Waw Alif) or `أواه`

* 3. Nasalization Engine

** Monosyllabic & Syllable-Final Nasals
Syllable-closing nasal vowels (*-m*, *-n*) neutralise to **Mīm** (`م`):
- *bom* $\rightarrow$ `بم`
- *bem* $\rightarrow$ `بم`
- *sim* $\rightarrow$ `سيم` / `سم`

** Nasal Diphthongs (-ão, -ãe, -õe)
Nasal diphthongs combine the nasal vowel stem with explicit vocalic glides:
- **-ão** $\rightarrow$ **ناو** (*Nūn-Alif-Waw*):
  - *não* $\rightarrow$ `ناو`
  - *cão* $\rightarrow$ `كناو`
  - Maintains clear distinction from *num* (`نم`) and *nem* (`نم`).
- **-ãe / -ães** $\rightarrow$ **ناي** / **نايها**:
  - *mãe* $\rightarrow$ `ماي`
  - *pães* $\rightarrow$ `پايها` / `پانيها`
- **-õe / -ões** $\rightarrow$ **نژو** / **نژوها** or **كونونجوها**:
  - *convenção* $\rightarrow$ `كونونجو`
  - *convenções* $\rightarrow$ `كونونجوها`

* 4. Morphophonemic Rules & Domain Mapping

** Rule I: Nominal Number Domain (-hā / ـها)
The suffix **-hā** (`ـها`) is strictly reserved as a graphic logogram for **nominal $[+\text{PL}]$ number inflection**. It attaches to:
1. **Nouns**: *âmbitos* $\rightarrow$ `آمبيتها`, *livros* $\rightarrow$ `ليوروها`
2. **Adjectives**: *boas* $\rightarrow$ `بوها` / `اهابوها`, *bons* $\rightarrow$ `بنها`
3. **Determiners & Articles**: *as* $\rightarrow$ `اها` (*a* + *hā*), *os* $\rightarrow$ `وها` (*o* + *hā*)
4. **3rd-Person Accusative Clitics**: *fazê-los* $\rightarrow$ `فازيلوها` (*fazê-lo* + *hā*)

** Rule II: Direct Surface Substitution
Morphophonological alternations in Portuguese plurals are applied **first** to produce the surface spoken form, after which Latin `-s` is directly replaced by `ـها`:
- *plural* $\rightarrow$ *plurais* $\rightarrow$ **پلورايها**
- *real* $\rightarrow$ *reais* $\rightarrow$ **ريايها**
- *animal* $\rightarrow$ *animais* $\rightarrow$ **انيمايها**
- *bom* $\rightarrow$ *bons* $\rightarrow$ **بنها** (nasal assimilation $m \to n$ before suffixation)

** Rule III: Verbal & Lexical Sibilant Domain (س / ز)
The letters **Sīn** (`س`) and **Zāy** (`ز`) are preserved for non-nominal sibilants:
1. **2nd-Person Verbal Agreement**: *tu fazes* $\rightarrow$ `فازس`, *tu tinhas* $\rightarrow$ `تينياس`
2. **Inherent Pronouns & Reflexive Clitics**: *se* $\rightarrow$ `سه`, *nos* (clitic) $\rightarrow$ `نوس`
3. **Lexical Root Sibilants**: *sim* $\rightarrow$ `سيم`, *mês* $\rightarrow$ `ميس`, *paz* $\rightarrow$ `پاز`

* 5. Edge Cases & Special Constructions

** Preposition + Article Contractions
Contractions of prepositions (*de*, *em*, *por*) with articles follow the nominal plural rule:
- **do** $\rightarrow$ `دو` | **dos** $\rightarrow$ `دوها` (*do* + *hā*)
- **da** $\rightarrow$ `ده` / `دا` | **das** $\rightarrow$ `دها` / `داها` (*da* + *hā*)
- **no** $\rightarrow$ `نو` | **nos** (preposition + article) $\rightarrow$ **نوها** (*no* + *hā*)
  - *Crucial Distinction*: Prepositional *nos* (`نوها`) vs. 1st-person object clitic *nos* (`نوس`).
- **pelo** $\rightarrow$ `پلو` | **pelos** $\rightarrow$ `پلوها`

** Lexical Singulars in -s / -z vs. Plural Inflections
- Singular lexical roots retain explicit `س` or `ز`:
  - *mês* (month) $\rightarrow$ `ميس`
  - *paz* (peace) $\rightarrow$ `پاز`
  - *luz* (light) $\rightarrow$ `لوز`
- Their plural inflections take nominal `-hā`:
  - *meses* $\rightarrow$ `ميزها` (*mês* stem + *hā*)
  - *luzes* $\rightarrow$ `لوزها` / `لوزيها`

** Hiatus vs. Diphthongs (país vs. pais)
- **Diphthongs**: *pais* (parents, plural of *pai*) $\rightarrow$ `پايها` (*pai* + *hā*)
- **Hiatus with tonic vowel**: *país* (country) $\rightarrow$ `پائيس` / `پاإيس` (marked with Hamzah `ئ` or Tonic Alif)

** Clitic Attachment & Hyphenation
- **Accusative 3rd-person plurals**: *fazê-los* $\rightarrow$ `فازيلوها`
- **Reflexive / 1st-person clitics**: *disse-se* $\rightarrow$ `ديسيسه`, *damo-nos* $\rightarrow$ `دامونوس`
- **Indirect object clitics**: *dar-lhe* $\rightarrow$ `دارليه`, *dar-lhes* $\rightarrow$ `دارليها`

** Consonant Clusters
Clusters without intervening vowels (*pr*, *tr*, *cl*, *br*, *pt*, *ct*) are written in direct succession:
- *prática* $\rightarrow$ `پراكتيكه`
- *apto* $\rightarrow$ `اپتو`

* 7. Corpus & Sample Transcriptions

Below are interlinear transcriptions of classic foundational sentences into Aljamiado Português:

1. **Sentence 1**: *Equilíbrio é muito importante para a forma de escrever.*
   - **Aljamiado**: `إكيليبكيو إ̷ مويتو إمپورتانته پارا ا فورمه دو إسكرور.`

2. **Sentence 2**: *As coisas boas.*
   - **Aljamiado**: `اها كويزها بوها`

3. **Sentence 3**: *O livro bom e os livros bons.*
   - **Aljamiado**: `و ليورو بم و وها ليوروها بنها`

4. **Sentence 4**: *Consegui conversar com o Gemini sobre o projeto do português no alfabeto persa, ou melhor, uma adaptação.*
   - **Aljamiado**: `كونسيگى كونورنار كم و جيمينى سوبره و پرژتو دو پرتوگيس نو الفبتو پرسه، او مليور، اومه ادپتاجو.`

5. **Sentence 5**: *Boa sorte com os estudos! O processo de criar uma ortografia é fascinante, e muito bom ver como o sistema tá se desenvolvendo.*
   - **Aljamiado**: `بوه سورته كم وها إستودوها! و پروچسو دو كزيار اومه اورتوگرافيه إ̷ فاسينانته، و مويتو بم وهر كومو و سيستمه تا سه ديزينوولويندو.`
